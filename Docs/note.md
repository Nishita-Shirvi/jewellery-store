# Notes

Extra information that doesn't belong in goals, progress or blockers: decisions, defaults, domain knowledge, dummy data, and useful references.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Project in one line

An online jewellery store (gold, silver, diamond and fashion jewellery) for the Indian market, built backend-first with Next.js, PostgreSQL/Prisma, Auth.js and Razorpay.

---

## 2. Frontend approach (until client inputs arrive)

- **Backend comes first.** Every phase finishes its services, APIs, database and tests completely.
- **Frontend is a placeholder.** Plain pages using shadcn/ui default styling, only enough to click through flows and test the backend.
- **Dummy data and images.** Products and categories live in `prisma/seed-data/catalog.ts` (used by the placeholder home page now and by the Phase 2 seed script). Each product has a dummy illustration in `public/images/products/<slug>.svg`.
- **Keep the UI swappable.** Components receive typed data from `services/` and contain no business logic, so the final design can replace them without touching the backend.
- **Write down data contracts.** For each page, note what data the service returns (types in `types/`) so the designer knows what is available.
- The final design is tracked as the "Future phase — Final frontend design" in `Goal.md`.

## 4. Jewellery domain notes

These affect the data model and rules. Items marked ⚠️ must be checked with a CA or lawyer before launch.

- **Purity:** gold is sold as 24K (999), 22K (916), 18K (750), 14K (585). Silver is usually 925 (sterling).
- **Metal colour:** yellow, white and rose gold.
- **Weight:** gross weight (whole piece) and net weight (metal only, without stones). Price for gold items is usually based on net weight.
- **Typical price build-up (if dynamic pricing is chosen later):** metal rate × net weight + making charges + stone value + GST.
- **Hallmarking:** BIS hallmarking with a 6-digit HUID is mandatory for most gold jewellery sold in India. Store the HUID per item and show it on the product page and invoice. ⚠️
- **GST:** jewellery is commonly taxed at 3% (HSN 7113). ⚠️ Making charges and stand-alone stones may be treated differently — confirm.
- **High-value purchases:** PAN may be required above certain purchase amounts, and cash payments above certain limits are restricted. This matters for COD. ⚠️
- **Sizes:** Indian ring sizes (roughly 6–30), bangle sizes (2.2, 2.4, 2.6, 2.8 inches), chain lengths (16, 18, 20, 22 inches). These become variant options in Phase 17.
- **Shipping:** high-value parcels need insured shipping; not all couriers accept precious items (see blocker B-05).
- **Stock:** many pieces are one-of-a-kind (stock = 1), so overselling protection in Phase 9 is important.
- **Search terms customers use:** jhumka, kada, mangalsutra, chandbali, solitaire, nose pin, anklet/payal, 22k, 916.
